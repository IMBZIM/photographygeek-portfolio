The upfront deposit secures the camera equipment and locks in your event date. The remaining balance is confirmed with your booking details.

What is included in event coverage? +

Event coverage includes professional camera gear, transport and logistics, full coverage of the agreed time, and a high-resolution digital gallery.

How much does coverage cost? +

Roora and event coverage is $50 per hour. Contact me with your date, location, and estimated coverage time for availability confirmation.

When will I receive my photos? +

Delivery timing depends on the size and type of session. Your expected gallery timeline will be confirmed when your booking is arranged.

How do I check availability? +

Complete the booking form with your name, event type, and project details. I will reply with availability and next steps.
